About Moove

Location Aware Systems – Revolutionising Fleet Management in Ethiopia and Beyond

Moove Fleet Management Platform is a cutting‑edge, locally developed solution that integrates with stationary and mobile assets to provide real‑time intelligence for government, NGOs, and private enterprises. Our mission is to enhance security, optimise fuel usage, and drive economic growth by reducing reliance on foreign currency for fleet management services.

Who We Are

We are a team of Ethiopian technologists and logistics experts who saw the urgent need for a home‑grown fleet management system after the post‑war challenges in Tigray. Inspired by the success of our deployment with Goal Ethiopia and other international aid organisations, we built a platform that is not only cost‑effective but also tailored to the unique realities of African transport and security.

Our primary goal goes beyond mere fleet optimisation – we actively contribute to Ethiopia’s national strategy of conserving foreign exchange by offering a competitive, locally hosted alternative to foreign subscription‑based systems.

What We Do

  • Real‑time Vehicle Tracking – using GPS protocols like GT06/N, UT04S, and others common in Ethiopia.
  • Fuel Misuse Prevention – detect anomalies, track consumption trends, and receive alerts for fuel drop or increase.
  • Security & Theft Protection – instant notifications for unauthorised movement, geofence breaches, and ignition events.
  • Government & Aid Collaboration – trusted by organisations that require transparency and accountability in aid distribution.
  • Post‑War Safety Monitoring – critical for assets operating in conflict‑affected zones, providing peace of mind.

Our Impact

Since our launch, we have helped organisations reduce fuel theft by up to 30%, lower maintenance costs through predictive alerts, and improve response times for emergency services. Our platform integrates seamlessly with the government’s electronic fuel payment initiative, ensuring that every litre is accounted for from distributor to end‑user.

Our Vision

To become the leading fleet management provider across Africa, setting the standard for transparency, efficiency, and local innovation. We aim to expand to other African nations, creating a sustainable ecosystem that keeps foreign currency within the continent while improving road safety and asset security.
